How to play

Rentvine word ladder

Get from today's starting word to RENT in as few steps as possible — changing exactly one letter at a time.

  • 1Each guess must be a real 4‑letter word.
  • 2It must differ from the word before it by exactly one letter.
  • 3Keep going until you reach RENT — there's no limit on guesses, but fewer is better.
  • 4A new puzzle unlocks every day.
